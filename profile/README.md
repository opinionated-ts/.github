# Opinionated TS

A collection of opinionated tools, configs, and templates for modern TypeScript development.

Built around a simple idea:

> Provide a modern and reliable foundation for TypeScript projects.

This organization provides reusable foundations for TypeScript projects, including development environments, tooling, configurations, and best practices.

## Philosophy

- Modern defaults
- Strong type safety
- Simple configuration
- Fast developer experience
- Flexible and customizable foundations

Designed to provide a complete and opinionated development experience, with carefully selected tools, workflows, and configurations that can be adapted when needed.

## Projects

- [`config`](https://github.com/opinionated-ts/config) — Reusable TypeScript development configurations and tooling.
- [`template`](https://github.com/opinionated-ts/template) — TypeScript project template based on the shared configuration.
- [`ai-tools`](https://github.com/opinionated-ts/ai-tools) — Tools, skills, and workflows for AI-assisted development.
- [`ai-spec`](https://github.com/opinionated-ts/ai-spec) — An open specification for conventions around AI-assisted development.

Projects are developed independently and may be at different stages of development.

> [!WARNING]
> Most projects are currently experimental or under active development. Their APIs, configuration, and scope may change as the projects evolve.
